## AI, both shield and catalyst

While AI serves as our shield, it is also a catalyst for attackers: attack automation, phishing message writing, and vulnerability research.

<div className="blog-slides">
  <img src="/img/blog/zeroday_clock-4.png" alt="3 times more clicks on AI-generated emails" />
</div>

## The Mythos paradox

Mozilla's recent experience with Claude Mythos (Anthropic's model) perfectly illustrates this paradox: the model allowed them to find more vulnerabilities in one month than their teams used to find in a year.

So yes, that's great, because it makes our software more and more secure, but the fixes for these vulnerabilities (especially when they are public, as in open source) are valuable information for attackers. They can use them to attack users who have not yet updated their application, or similar software that has not yet applied those patches.

## What now?

This situation is worrying and will challenge our defenses and best practices over the coming years. In the meantime, let's not forget to update our applications!
